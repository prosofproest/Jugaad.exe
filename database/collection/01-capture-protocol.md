# Capture protocol

**Protocol version:** `v1.0-draft`
**Status:** Draft for agronomist and field-operations approval. **Not yet issued to collectors.**
**Owner:** Field operations lead (TBD), with Agronomist/pathologist (TBD)
**Last updated:** 2026-11-06
**Authorities:** `final/docs-context/data-foundation.md` §3;
`final/docs-context/phase-plan.md` §4.2;
`final/feature-specs/05-data-layer.md` §4.3, §8;
`final/docs-context/AGENTS.md` R1–R10.

> **What this document is:** the standard way to photograph a crop problem so the images
> represent real farmer submissions and can be audited.
>
> **What it is not:** a diagnostic guide, a treatment guide, or permission to give chemical
> advice. It contains no pesticide names, doses, mixtures or intervals, and collectors must
> never provide them (R5).

---

## 1. Scope and definitions

| Term | Definition used here |
|---|---|
| **Case** | One observation: one plant or one affected area, at one place and time, submitted under one `client_report_id`. A case may contain several images. |
| **Shot** | One image within a case, with a defined purpose (`shot_type`). |
| **Collector** | Enumerator or extension worker capturing on their own behalf or on behalf of a farmer (`subject_user_id`). |
| **Farmer submission** | A case captured by the farmer directly through the app. |
| **Re-take** | A shot discarded at capture time because it failed the self-check, before upload. |
| **Quarantine** | A system or reviewer rejection after upload. Governed by `../governance/06-quarantine-policy.md`, not by this protocol. |

**One case is not one image.** A case with four shots of the same plant is still one case
for sampling and splitting. Counting images instead of cases inflates progress and leaks
near-duplicates across train/test.

---

## 2. Rules that override convenience

1. **Never substitute a stock, downloaded or borrowed image.** Every image must be captured
   by the submitting user, at the stated place and time.
2. **Never re-use an old image for a new case.** A follow-up visit is a new case.
3. **Never photograph people, faces, identity documents, house numbers, number plates or
   screens.** If a person is in frame, re-take.
4. **Never let the app attach precise GPS to the image file.** EXIF GPS is stripped unless
   exact location is separately consented; the app records the coarse `geo_code` instead.
5. **Never edit, crop, filter or annotate the original image.** Derivatives are generated by
   the pipeline from the untouched original.
6. **Never diagnose or advise treatment.** The collector records what is visible and what the
   farmer reports. A qualified reviewer assigns the label.
7. **A failed shot is re-taken, not deleted and forgotten.** Re-takes are counted so the
   pilot can measure the protocol's reject rate.
8. **Offline capture is normal.** A case must survive no connectivity, an app restart and a
   dead battery without losing the images or the metadata.

## 3. Devices

### 3.1 Approved devices

| Requirement | Rule | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Rear camera with autofocus | Required |
| Resolution | Minimum capture resolution | `TBD` — set with D1 and device trial |
| OS | Supported Android version | `TBD` — set with the mobile build |
| App | Official project app only | Required; no third-party camera app |
| Storage | Free space for the day's queue | `TBD` — set per pilot day |
| Battery | Enough for a full collection day | `TBD` — carry power bank |

**Device classes.** Every case records a `device_class`. The class boundaries are `TBD`
until the device trial, but the intended bands are:

| Code | Intended band |
|---|---|
| `DEV-LOW` | Entry-level Android, the cheapest device the pilot supports |
| `DEV-MID` | Mid-range Android, the common farmer device |
| `DEV-HIGH` | Higher-end Android |

A dataset collected only on high-end phones will not represent farmer submissions. The
sampling matrix sets device quotas.

### 3.2 Camera settings

- Flash **off** by default. Flash glare hides lesion colour and texture.
- HDR **off**. HDR can merge frames and distort colour.
- Digital zoom **off**. Move closer instead; zoom destroys resolution.
- Grid lines **on**, to keep the subject level.
- Tap to focus on the affected area before every close-up.
- Clean the lens before each case. A smudge is indistinguishable from blur.
- Do not use beauty modes, filters, or automatic "enhance".

## 4. The shot list

Every case has a **minimum of three shots**, in this order. The maximum is `TBD`
(proposed 8) to stop near-duplicate flooding.

| # | `shot_type` | What it shows | Distance | Angle | Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | `context` | Whole plant, or the affected part in its canopy | 1.0–2.0 m | Horizontal to slightly above | Yes |
| S2 | `mid` | The affected plant part with some surrounding tissue | 0.3–0.5 m | 45–90° to the surface | Yes |
| S3 | `closeup` | The affected area filling the frame | 0.1–0.2 m | Perpendicular to the surface | Yes |
| S4 | `underside` | Lower leaf surface, for leaf symptoms | 0.1–0.2 m | Perpendicular | Required for leaf symptoms, otherwise optional |
| S5 | `field_context` | Row or block context | 3–5 m | Horizontal | Optional; no people or identifiers |

Distance bands are **draft defaults**. They must be confirmed per crop in the pilot before
the protocol is issued as v1.1. The app enforces nothing about physical distance; the
collector records a `distance_class` and the supervisor spot-checks it.

### 4.1 Shot rules

- **S1 context** must include enough of the plant that the reviewer can tell what part is
  affected. Do not fill the frame with a single leaf for S1.
- **S3 close-up** must fill at least roughly half the frame with the affected area. Include
  healthy tissue at the edge for contrast where possible.
- **S4 underside** is required when the symptom is on a leaf, because many conditions look
  different from above and below.
- **Do not submit five near-identical close-ups.** Each shot has a different job. Duplicate
  shots are quarantined by perceptual hash and waste reviewer time.
- **Shoot the range, not the worst case only.** At the start of an outbreak, early symptoms
  are the most valuable images. Photograph first signs, not just advanced damage.

## 5. Lighting

| Code | Condition | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `L-SUN` | Direct sunlight | Shoot with the sun behind or to the side of the collector; avoid the collector's shadow across the subject |
| `L-SHADE` | Open shade | Preferred for harsh midday sun; keep colour neutral |
| `L-OVERCAST` | Cloud / diffuse | Good for even colour; watch for motion blur in low light |
| `L-INDOOR` | Indoors / ambient lamp | Allowed but must be recorded; mixed colour temperature is a known domain |
| `L-MIXED` | Mixed sun and shade | Record it; avoid dappled light across the lesion |
| `L-FLASH` | Flash used | Discouraged; must be recorded and flagged if used |

Rules:

- Prefer daylight, but **do not refuse a real farmer image because the light is poor**.
  Record the condition instead.
- Avoid shooting into the sun (backlight) and avoid deep shadow.
- If the subject is in deep shade and the image is unusable, re-take in open shade.
- Never use flash to "brighten" a close-up; it creates glare that hides texture.

## 6. Background

Background is a real part of the domain. Laboratory images on plain white backgrounds do not
represent farmer photos.

| Code | Background | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `B-SOIL` | Bare soil | Common field background |
| `B-MULCH` | Mulch / straw / residue | |
| `B-CROP` | Other crop tissue | |
| `B-HAND` | Collector's hand supporting the leaf | Allowed; no jewellery, no visible identifiers |
| `B-STRUCTURE` | Wall, fence, tool, vehicle | Avoid number plates and house numbers |
| `B-MIXED` | Mixed natural background | |
| `B-PLAIN` | Deliberately plain background | Allowed only as an extra shot, never as the only shot |

Rules:

- Keep the natural background for S1 and S2. Do not stage every image against a plain sheet.
- Remove clutter that identifies a person or a household.
- A hand is acceptable support; a face is not.

## 7. Plant part and plant age

### 7.1 Plant part

| Code | Part |
|---|---|
| `PP-LEAF` | Leaf (record upper/lower via `shot_type` and notes) |
| `PP-STEM` | Stem / tiller / branch |
| `PP-FRUIT` | Fruit / pod / grain head |
| `PP-FLOWER` | Flower / inflorescence |
| `PP-ROOT` | Root / tuber / collar |
| `PP-WHOLE` | Whole plant |

Record the part that carries the symptom. If several parts are affected, submit separate
cases or record the primary part and note the others.

### 7.2 Plant age

Record the growth stage at capture, using the crop-specific stage set from the crop calendar.
The stage list is **`TBD — D1`**. Until it exists, record the closest available stage and a
`plant_age_days` estimate if the farmer knows the planting date. Never guess a planting date.

---

## 8. Severity

**Record severity before photographing.** The field assessment is evidence; the image is
not a substitute for it.

- Severity uses the **crop-specific visual criteria** agreed with the agronomist.
  A generic percentage scale is **not** used unless it has been validated for that crop and
  symptom (`final/docs-context/data-foundation.md` §4.4).
- The scale itself is **`TBD — D1`**. Proposed ordinal bands, pending validation:
  `SEV-0` none / healthy, `SEV-1` first signs, `SEV-2` clear symptoms, `SEV-3` advanced,
  `SEV-4` severe / necrotic / dieback.
- Record what is visible: lesion count or coverage where the validated scale requires it,
  and which plant parts are affected.
- Do not compute severity from the photo after the fact and overwrite the field value.
  A later reviewer may assign an adjudicated severity, and the original is kept.

Severity is a **sampling dimension**, not just a field. The matrix requires the full range,
including healthy controls and early symptoms.

---

## 9. Multiple images per case

- Minimum 3 shots; maximum `TBD` (proposed 8).
- All shots in a case must be from the **same plant/area and the same visit**.
- Order the shots S1 → S5. The app stores `shot_index`.
- Do not mix two plants into one case. If two plants are affected, create two cases.
- Do not attach a previous visit's image to a follow-up case.
- The case is the unit for deduplication, sampling and splitting. Duplicate images within a
  case are expected; duplicate cases are not.

**Follow-up visits.** A re-visit is a **new case** linked to the same `growing_area_id` and
optionally to the earlier observation. It is not an edit of the earlier case. Linking is
`TBD` in the schema; record the earlier case ID in `notes` until a link field exists.

### 10.3 Not recorded in image metadata

- Collector or farmer name.
- Phone number, email or national ID.
- Exact GPS coordinates inside EXIF.
- Free-text notes containing personal information.
- Any biometric or face template.

If exact location is ever collected, it is a separate, explicitly consented field stored in
the database, never embedded in the image and never exposed in exports.

## 11. Before-upload self-check

The app presents this checklist; the collector must confirm it. The server re-checks the
mechanical items.

- [ ] Lens clean; image sharp on the affected area.
- [ ] Not over-exposed, not in deep shadow, no flash glare.
- [ ] Plant and affected area clearly visible.
- [ ] S1, S2 and S3 present; S4 present for leaf symptoms.
- [ ] No people, faces, documents, house numbers or number plates.
- [ ] Correct crop, stage, severity and plant part selected.
- [ ] No stock, borrowed, screenshot or edited image.
- [ ] Consent recorded for this case.

A failed self-check is a **re-take**, not a submission. Re-takes are logged.

---

## 12. Capture failures and re-takes

| Failure | Action | Counted as |
|---|---|---|
| Out of focus | Re-take | Capture re-take |
| Too dark / glare | Re-take in better light | Capture re-take |
| Plant not visible | Re-take | Capture re-take |
| Person in frame | Re-take | Privacy re-take |
| Wrong crop/stage selected | Correct metadata, not an image re-take | Metadata correction |
| Duplicate shot | Delete locally before upload | Not submitted |
| Upload fails | Stay queued, retry | Upload failure |
| App crash | Recover from local queue | Incident |
| Wrong image from gallery | Remove, capture fresh | Provenance risk |

**A re-take is not a hidden deletion.** The app records the attempt count per shot type so
the pilot can compute a capture reject rate.

---

## 13. Offline-first behaviour

- Capture and metadata entry work with no connectivity.
- Every case is written to a local durable queue before the UI confirms success.
- Upload is idempotent on `client_report_id` and per-image `content_hash`.
- A case is not marked submitted until every image is confirmed by the server.
- If the device runs out of space, the app stops accepting new cases and warns the
  collector; it never silently drops the oldest.
- If a queued case is deleted locally, the deletion is recorded and reported; it is not a
  silent loss.
- Sync conflicts are resolved in favour of the server for status, and in favour of the
  device for a not-yet-uploaded original.

---

## 14. Protocol versioning

- This document is versioned. Every case records the `protocol_version` used.
- A material change (shot list, distance bands, required metadata, lighting rules) bumps the
  version and triggers re-training.
- A minor wording change that does not alter capture behaviour may be a patch version with a
  change-log entry.
- Old protocol versions remain readable; cases are never re-labelled to a new protocol.
- The pilot may produce `v1.1`; the released field protocol is at least `v1.1` before scale-up.

| Version | Date | Change | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v1.0-draft` | 2026-11-06 | Initial draft | Not approved |

## 15. Pre-issue checklist

The protocol may be issued to collectors only when all of these are true:

- [ ] Crop list, target list and severity scale are set (D1).
- [ ] Crop-specific stage sets exist (D1).
- [ ] Distance bands and device minimums are confirmed in a device trial.
- [ ] Shot-type and metadata fields are reconciled with `05-data-layer.md`.
- [ ] Consent wording is approved and capture is live (`03-consent-wording-and-capture.md`).
- [ ] Permission for the pilot area is recorded (`01-dataset-register.md`).
- [ ] DPIA is signed (`04-dpia.md`).
- [ ] Enumerator training is complete (`03-enumerator-training-and-pilot.md`).
- [ ] Secure upload and storage are live (`04-secure-upload-and-retention.md`).
- [ ] Sampling matrix v1 is approved (`02-sampling-matrix.md`).

## 16. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every case has at least three shots with distinct `shot_type` values.
- [ ] `lighting`, `background`, `plant_part`, `distance_class`, `angle_class`,
      `device_class` and `protocol_version` are recorded for every image.
- [ ] Severity is recorded before the image is taken, not inferred from it.
- [ ] No case can be submitted without the required consent version.
- [ ] No original image is edited, cropped or filtered by the app.
- [ ] EXIF GPS is absent from stored originals unless separately consented.
- [ ] Re-takes and upload failures are counted, not hidden.
- [ ] A queued case survives app restart and connectivity loss.
- [ ] A stock, borrowed, screenshot or re-used image is detectable by provenance checks.
- [ ] The protocol version on each case matches a released protocol document.
